I'm Md Nazmul Islam, a PhD candidate in Mechatronics, Robotics and Automation Engineering at Kent State University in Kent, Ohio. I have been a graduate assistant here since August 2024. My advisor is Dr. Tao Shen.

I work on robotics, imaging systems, quantum sensors, computer vision, human-machine interfaces, control systems, machine learning, deep learning, healthcare AI, explainable AI, biomedical signal analysis, data mining, and IoT.

Before Kent State I was a field technical service specialist for Hugo Surgical Robotics at Medtronic, a field service engineer for CT and PET-CT at Philips Healthcare, an executive engineer for MRI and X-ray at Siemens Bangladesh Ltd., and a part-time lecturer in EEE at Daffodil International University.

I will write here about that work: medical and surgical robotics, sensing, and AI. The projects on this site are the public ones on my GitHub.
